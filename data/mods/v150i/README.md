# Improved

The 1.50 Improved rules: rebalanced race picks, stronger AI and Antarans, and revised tactical combat. This is what players usually mean by "playing 1.50". For single-player; use Improved Multiplayer for network games.

## Rules

Builds on Basic (v150) and changes many 1.31 rules. Everything below is relative to 1.31.

## What it changes

Races and setup:
- Race picks rebalanced: up to 12 negative picks (was 10); Unification costs 8, Feudal -6, Telepathic 8, Uncreative -6; High-G, Cybernetic, Fantastic Traders, Lucky, Stealthy Ships and Artifacts world cost less; Food and Income bonuses cost less; the Ship Attack bonus is +25 instead of +20.
- Home systems have at least 4 planets.
- AI races get corrected designs, revised personalities and extra abilities on Hard and Impossible.
- Galaxy generation is retuned (star mix, planet counts and climates, richer white stars, fewer wormholes); natives in monster systems get better climates.
- Gold and gem deposit planets are never Poor or Ultra Poor and have normal gravity.
- Uncreative races get repaired technology choices at game start, and Lithovores never get Weather Control.

Economy and colonies:
- The first colonist on a new colony is always a worker; new population farms only where farming pays well.
- Governments: Dictatorship has a smaller morale penalty and barracks bonus; Democracy and Federation get the Psionics morale bonus and smaller spy penalties; Federation's research and income bonus drops to Democracy's level but it gains +20% morale and no mixed-race penalty; Confederation's ship discount becomes 50%.
- A Recyclotron makes a Toxic colony count as Barren; Biomorphic Fungi helps farmable planets instead of dead worlds; food replicators are cheaper.
- Tiny Toxic, Radiated, Barren, Desert, Tundra and Ocean planets hold 2 population instead of 1; Tolerant races hold 1 less on some planets.
- Natives and a colony's last population can be moved; up to 50 population can be in transit.
- Androids cost twice as much (100 PP) and ignore morale.
- Many building costs and upkeeps change (cheaper barracks and farms, a 1000 PP Dimensional Portal).
- Research beyond twice the field cost carries over after a breakthrough; spies can steal any technology.
- Leader hire prices are re-weighted by skill, and Commando officers give defending ground troops a much larger bonus.
- Advanced Robotics and Advanced Manufacturing swap places in the tree; Phase Shifter becomes a Xenon technology.

Ships and combat:
- Strict ship initiative is always on: each ship may wait once per turn, and ships that waited act in reverse initiative order.
- Ship statistics and turn order update during the combat turn as damage, cloaking and captures happen.
- Defensive fire is on, but only against ships that come to close range; long-range beams use a simplified accuracy formula.
- Missiles and torpedoes move at the end of each turn, and volleys from different turns don't merge.
- Boarding range is 1 square (was 3); Enveloping weapons don't multiply damage against planets; the Capitol can be bombed.
- Many weapon, shield and special system sizes and costs are revised; a x3 missile rack is added; extra structural damage is +50% instead of +100%.
- Outpost ships cost twice as much; colony, outpost and transport ships carry your best stealth device.
- Stealth Field and cloaks are reworked; larger hulls have more shield points; planet and starbase defenses are revised.
- In automatic combat your fleets retreat by the AI's rules.

AI, monsters and events:
- New AI ship designs; the AI earns +1 BC per population on Impossible.
- Antarans attack earlier, with elite crews and new ship designs.
- Monsters appear earlier, are tougher and stay longer; the Guardian and Avenger are re-armed.
- Random events: Hyperspace Flux and Warp Beast wait until turn 100; anomalies, fluxes and warp beasts end sooner; pirate raids take less; Lucky races get events more often.
- Combat confirmation messages close after about 50 seconds.

## Keys

- W (wait): each ship can wait only once per combat turn; the Wait button greys out after use.
- H (wait with all ships) is available in every battle, since ship initiative is always on, until the ships that waited start to act.

## Needs and conflicts

Needs Basic (v150). Conflicts with Version 1.2 (v120), Melee, ICE and VDC-GM3. In multiplayer, use Improved Multiplayer instead; every player needs the same package set, and its scripts must match on all machines.

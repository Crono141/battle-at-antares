# Basic

The interface conveniences of the 1.50 patch, corrected game texts, and a few small rule tweaks. Most of the other 1.50-style packages build on it.

## Rules

Without packages the game plays the 1.31 rules with bug fixes. Basic keeps those rules almost unchanged: it is not the full 1.50 experience. For that, enable Improved (v150i), which includes Basic.

## What it changes

Interface:
- Faster screen changes: vertical sync is off and screens fade in and out four times as fast.
- The main menu lists the loaded packages; the original animated credits move to a separate Credits screen.
- The main galaxy screen shows your tax rate above the treasury when it is not zero.
- The Empire Info panel on the Colonies screen shows your chance of a research breakthrough.
- No warning when Housing, Trade Goods or Repeat Build sits in front of other items in a build queue.
- History graphs show the raw, unsmoothed data.
- Serious messages are listed first in the turn summary.
- Faster cloaking animation.
- A leader always shouts as his ship is destroyed, whatever destroyed it.

Ship design:
- The DESIGN button on the build screen keeps a design's chosen shield and computer instead of switching to the best available.
- The CLEAR button on the design screen updates the shield to the best available.
- New missile weapons default to the x2 rack instead of x5.

Rules:
- Population grown while a colony builds Housing never becomes farmers.
- Research points are kept when the technology you are researching is gained another way (artifacts, spying, capture).
- Comets, supernovas and space-time anomalies can't happen before turn 150. Comets and supernovas can come earlier than before (turn 200 in 1.31), anomalies later (turn 50).

Texts and graphics:
- Corrected help pages, technology names and descriptions, leader skill texts, event messages and other game messages.
- Matching message fixes for the French, German, Italian and Spanish versions.
- Some race-selection and pop-up graphics are replaced with corrected versions.

## Needs and conflicts

No dependencies or conflicts. Improved (v150i), and through it Improved Multiplayer (v150i-mp), require Basic and enable it automatically. In multiplayer every player needs the same package set.

## Saves

Saves record this package and need it enabled to load.

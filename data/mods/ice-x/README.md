# ICE-X

ICE-X is the hardest version of ICE: it keeps every ICE rule and makes the AI empires and the Antarans much stronger. It is meant for experienced single players who find ICE too easy.

## Rules

ICE-X changes nothing about races, the map, the economy or combat rules; all of that comes from ICE, which in turn builds on the Basic 1.50 package (v150). ICE-X only replaces ICE's difficulty profile.

## What it changes

### AI and difficulty
- AI colonies get a larger output bonus at every level: +1/2 production and research per worker on Easy, +1 on Average, +2 on Hard and +3 on Impossible, plus half as much food. ICE gives none on Easy and +2 on Impossible.
- On Hard and Impossible the AI races use ICE's strongest racial variants (variants 4 and 5), which add more extra traits than the variants ICE normally uses.

### Antarans
- Antaran resources grow every 12 turns instead of every 20, and their attack roll is shorter, so they strike more often.
- Larger reserves: up to 6 Raiders in the attack fleets, and up to 6 Intruders, 10 Interdictors and 16 Harbingers defending Antares.
- Defending Antaran frigates carry no weapons, but on Hard and Impossible they have a Damper Field, Phasing Cloak and Warp Dissipator.
- Antaran battleships and titans carry more Particle Beams and Death Rays.
- The Antaran Star Fortress gets more weapon space (2500 instead of 2000) and adds a Sub-Space Teleporter and an Achilles Targeting Unit.

## Keys

Inherited from ICE (this package changes no keys itself):

| Key | Screen | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| W | Tactical combat | Each ship can wait only once per combat turn. Ships that waited act afterwards, in reverse initiative order, and W is not available during that second pass. |
| H | Tactical combat | Waits with all your ships. Ship initiative is always on (the in-game Ship Initiative option can't switch it off), so H is there in every battle until the waiting ships start their second pass. |
| Alt+A, Alt+D | Build | Auto Design still redesigns every ship, but follows ICE's AI design rules, so the designs differ. |
| Alt + MENLO | Main | The research cheat grants 100,000 research points instead of 1,000,000. |

## Needs and conflicts

- Requires ICE 35 and loads after it; ICE and Basic 1.50 (v150) are enabled automatically.
- Inherits ICE's conflicts: it cannot be combined with Improved (v150i), the Version 1.2 mod (v120) or VDC-GM3.
- ICE-X itself has no scripts, but ICE does, so in multiplayer every player needs the same versions of ICE and ICE-X.

## Saves

Saved games record this package, and it must be installed to load them.
